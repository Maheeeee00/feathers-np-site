About Feathers NutriPharma
Introduction

Two Divisions. One Health-Focused Company.

Feathers NutriPharma (Pvt) Ltd is a nutraceutical company working across human and veterinary health.

Our veterinary health journey began with nutritional solutions for companion animals and pet birds. Building on our scientific and veterinary background, we are now expanding into human health, developing and introducing specialized nutritional solutions for emerging healthcare needs.

Our approach is based on

  • Scientific formulation
  • Targeted nutritional support
  • Quality-focused development
  • Veterinary and healthcare expertise
  • Research and innovation
  • Responsible health communication
