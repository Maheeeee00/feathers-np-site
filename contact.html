FAQ

Common questions.

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message and our team will help.

Where can I buy Feathers Nutripharma products?

Our products are available through pet shops and online bird‑supply stores across Pakistan. Contact us to find a stockist near you.

How do I give the supplements to my birds?

Most of our powders and syrups mix into drinking water or soft food. Always follow the dosage printed on the pack, and ask a vet if your bird is unwell.

Can I use more than one supplement at a time?

Many keepers combine products, for example electrolytes in summer with a regular multivitamin. Contact us for guidance so you don’t over‑supplement.

How do I become a distributor or stockist?

Fill in the contact form and choose “Distributor / Pet shop”. Our team will share trade pricing and terms.
