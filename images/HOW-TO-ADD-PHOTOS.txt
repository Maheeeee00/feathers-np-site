HOW TO ADD REAL PHOTOS
======================

1. Open https://feathersnp.com and each product page.
2. Right-click the product photo -> "Save image as..."
3. Rename it EXACTLY as shown below and put it in the right folder.
   .jpg, .png or .webp all work.
4. Upload to GitHub. Vercel updates the site automatically.

Any photo you don't add keeps showing the illustration, so the site never looks broken.
