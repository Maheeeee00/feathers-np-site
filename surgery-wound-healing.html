Surgery & Wound Healing

Specialized Nutritional Support for Recovery

Surgery and wound healing can increase the body’s nutritional requirements. Adequate nutritional support plays an important role in maintaining normal physiological functions and supporting the body’s natural recovery processes.

Feathers NutriPharma is developing specialized nutritional solutions focused on the nutritional needs associated with surgical recovery and wound healing.

Our Focus

  • Nutritional support during recovery
  • Support for tissue repair
  • Support for normal wound-healing processes
  • Nutritional support during increased physiological demand
  • Recovery-focused supplementation
  • Professional healthcare guidance
