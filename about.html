About Feathers NutriPharma

Feathers NutriPharma (Pvt) Ltd is a Pakistan-based nutraceutical company working across human and veterinary health.

The company was established to develop and provide innovative nutritional solutions addressing practical health needs.

Our journey has its roots in veterinary science, diagnostics and animal health. Over time, this experience developed into a broader interest in nutraceutical research and targeted nutritional support.

As the company grows, our focus remains on science, quality, innovation and responsible healthcare.

Today, Feathers operates through two developing areas

Human Health DivisionSpecialized nutritional solutions for human healthcare and wellness.Veterinary Health DivisionNutraceutical solutions for companion animals and pet birds.
Vision

To build a trusted health and nutraceutical company connecting science, nutrition and innovation across human and veterinary health.

Mission

To develop and promote scientifically informed nutraceutical solutions that support better health, recovery, vitality and quality of life in humans and animals.
